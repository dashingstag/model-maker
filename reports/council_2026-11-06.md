## Harness/process defects surfaced by review → filed in BUG_LEDGER

- **#52** patroller frozen in walk texture for all static shots (overlap sim never restores `sprite_idle`) — every vista/pair shot shows cloud_step mid-stride.
- **#53** unresolved shot names silently decoy: `meditation_close`, `sword_salute`, `sleeve_dance` are byte-identical to `vista_all`; roster's `card_focus`, `element_swap` identical to `select_grid` — **and all three roster dupes are blessed goldens**.
- **#54** `salute_sync` capture camera `"terrace"` is not a CAP_CAMS name → mid fallback; second actor framed out.
- **#55** jade variant members absent from roster jobs `members` — element-swap intent unreachable by authoring.
- **#56/#57** prop identity failures certified despite must_have violations → C3 vision checklist never checks must_have semantics.
- **#58** pose-semantics regression class persists (no sword / not seated / wing-artifacts) — #13's fix covered structure but certified files still break pose names.
- **#59** 3D unit/turntable shots under-fill frame — subject-extent camera fit still missing (regression of #50 class).

## Recommendations — leverage order

1. **Re-certify `xx_sage_staff` + `xx_ribbon`** (`--recertify`): regenerate with vertical-orientation/shaft language strengthened; these two are the only outright certified-failures found.
2. **Add must_have semantic check to the C3 vision checklist** — "does the image contain X" per must_have token + pose-name verification. Cheap; would have caught every FAIL row above.
3. **Shot-resolution honesty**: `_setup_shot` should report unresolved shot names (`pass:null`-style) so decoys can't bless. Highest pipeline value per effort.
4. **Art-direction decision on floating props**: floating jian/artifacts beside cultivators is genre-correct for xianxia — either embrace (add slight self-glow so the float reads magical, not sloppy) or tighten attach offsets to hand contact. Currently it reads unintentional.
5. **Differentiate the two immortals at silhouette level**: m needs a dark/martial accent or sword-in-hand idle; f keeps gold/wide-sleeve. At vista zoom they are the same figure in two trims.
6. **Sprite-scene value integration**: white/gold sprites read pasted onto the dusk vista; a mild cool ambient grade on sprites (or warmer rig key) would seat them.
7. **Re-open #13's pose-semantics class** for immortal_m's sword_salute/meditate_lotus/cloud_step — either re-gen with guide, or rename poses to what they actually depict.
8. **Hero GLB presentation**: fit turntable/unit cameras to subject extent; tame stage-light blowout on the face; consider whether sleeves read as mitts at gameplay zoom (may be fine at 400px).
